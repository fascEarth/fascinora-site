Privacy policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Who we are

Fascinora is a remote-first software studio headquartered in India and serving clients worldwide. You can reach us at hello@fascinora.com.

What we collect

This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking scripts. The enquiry form does not send data anywhere: it prepares a message that you choose to copy and send to us. When you email us, we receive your name, contact details and whatever you write.

How we use it

We use your details only to reply to your enquiry, prepare a quote and deliver the work you ask for. We do not sell or share your information for marketing.

Demo apps

Our demos (AI Studio, StockFlow) run entirely in your browser. Anything you type stays on your device and is not sent to us.

Client project data

For client projects, business data is stored in the client’s own cloud accounts wherever possible, and is never used to train AI models. We sign an NDA on request.

Your rights

You can ask us to show, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by emailing hello@fascinora.com. We will reply within 30 days. Where data protection laws such as the GDPR apply to you, you have the rights those laws give you.

Fonts

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address as part of delivering the font files.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

If AI writes the code, is it reliable?

AI writes the first draft. A senior engineer reviews every change, runs the tests and approves it before release. You get the speed of AI with the judgement of an experienced developer.

Who owns the code and the data?

You do. The source code goes to your own GitHub account and the app runs in your own cloud account.

How fast can you deliver?

Websites usually take 1–2 weeks, chatbots 1–3 weeks and custom apps 3–8 weeks. Your quote states the timeline for your scope.

Where are your clients, and how do we work together?

Anywhere. We meet over Zoom or Google Meet, share a staging link every week and keep at least four hours of overlap with your working day.

What happens after launch?

You get 30 days of free fixes. After that, choose a monthly support plan or request changes as separate quotes.

Can my team edit the app ourselves?

If we build it on AI Studio, yes. Your team can add fields, forms and reports without writing code.

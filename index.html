How we work

From first message to launch

AI agents handle the repetitive work. People make every decision that matters: scope, price, architecture and release.

  1. You

    Share the problem

    Message us by email, the form below or WhatsApp. A voice note or a photo of your register is fine.

  2. Requirements agent + engineer

    Written scope

    Within one working day you get screens, features, assumptions and open questions in a short document.

  3. Engineer

    Fixed quote

    A fixed price and timeline. Nothing starts until you approve it. Changes are quoted separately.

  4. AI dev team

    Build in weekly steps

    Developer, reviewer and test agents build in small pieces. You get a staging link every week.

  5. Engineer

    Launch & handover

    Final review, deployment, training video, documentation and 30 days of free fixes.

Human in the loop, always. AI can write code fast, and it can also make confident mistakes. No AI-written change reaches your users until a senior engineer has read it, tested it and approved it.

FAQ

Questions clients ask

Something else on your mind? Ask in the form below and we will reply within one working day.

If AI writes the code, is it reliable?

AI writes the first draft. A senior engineer reviews every change, runs the tests and approves it before release. You get the speed of AI with the judgement of an experienced developer.

Who owns the code and the data?

You do. The source code is pushed to your own GitHub account and the app runs in your own cloud account. We keep nothing back.

How fast can you deliver?

Websites usually take 1–2 weeks, chatbots 1–3 weeks and custom apps 3–8 weeks. Your quote states the timeline for your scope.

Where are your clients, and how do we work together?

Anywhere. We work over Zoom or Google Meet, share progress on a weekly call and in a staging link, and keep at least four hours of overlap with your working day. Quotes are in USD by default; EUR, GBP, AED or INR on request.

What happens after launch?

You get 30 days of free bug fixes. After that, you can choose a monthly support plan or ask for changes as separate quotes.

Can my team edit the app ourselves?

If we build it on our low-code AI Studio, yes. Your team can add fields, forms and reports without writing code. Try the demo to see how it works.

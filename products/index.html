Nexus Core

A multi-tenant, event-driven application platform. Your next delivery app, HRMS, WMS or CRM is configured on it, not coded from scratch. The live demo is NexEats, a complete food-delivery business built on it.

  • Four connected apps: customer ordering, restaurant partner console, delivery rider app and admin console
  • Customers browse restaurants, use coupons, pay (simulated) and track the rider live on a map
  • Restaurants accept orders, mark food ready and manage menu and stock; riders get trips, pick up and drop
  • Order and delivery lifecycles are configurable workflows; alerts are no-code automation rules
  • Every page talks to every other: one action ripples to all screens instantly
  • Users, roles and permissions down to record and field level: each restaurant sees only its own orders
  • Live operations map, business insights, full audit trail and tenant isolation enforced in the database

MediConsult

A secure online doctor consultation platform for clinics, hospitals and health startups.

  • Patients find verified doctors and book slots in their own timezone
  • Video consultations in the browser, with in-call chat
  • e-Prescriptions, clinical notes and patient health records
  • Online payments with automatic refunds on cancellation
  • Doctor onboarding with registration verification by your admins
  • Health data encrypted at rest; every access audit-logged
  • Patient vitals tracking, doctor practice insights and an admin analytics console

Rideora

A white-label cab, van and chauffeur booking website with its own CMS portal. Each cab operator gets their own brand, prices, fleet and city.

  • Online booking for one-way, round-trip, hourly chauffeur and airport transfers
  • Fixed fares from distance, vehicle category, airport fees, night surcharges, extras, promo codes and tax
  • Customers manage or cancel their trip with a booking reference
  • CMS dashboard with revenue, upcoming trips and the vehicle mix
  • Dispatch board: confirm, assign drivers and vehicles, start and complete trips
  • Manage vehicles, drivers, rates, promotions, places, FAQ and all website text
  • Branding and region presets: business name, colour, currency, tax and service area
